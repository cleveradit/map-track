```markdown
## DEC-XXX — Short title

**Decision:** What was decided.

**Why:** Why — the constraint, bug, or wrong assumption.

**Impact:** Concrete consequences that are not obvious from the code.

**Tickets:** TICKET-XXX (optional)
```
